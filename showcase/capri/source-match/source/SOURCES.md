## Fonts and preview tools

Manrope, Inter and Martian Mono variable TrueType fonts were obtained from the Google Fonts repository at the exact file objects listed in assets/fonts/SOURCES.json. Downloaded byte sizes and Git blob IDs were checked; SHA-256 hashes are retained. Each family has its unchanged original OFL notice under its own folder. OFL.md is the original licence text with a Markdown filename, not a new licence assignment.

The standalone export helper and its supporting assets are frozen packet tooling under tools/vendor/visualize. They wrap the approved fragments and provide preview controls; they are not project runtime features. Its three pinned browser helper dependencies are inlined for offline export: Floating UI core 1.7.3 and DOM 1.7.4 (MIT), and Lucide 1.17.0 (ISC). Their original notices and downloaded-byte records are retained in tools/vendor/visualize/deps. Product fonts and required source assets are embedded in the exported pages. Keep the reference-led design policy folder complete when carrying this packet forward.
