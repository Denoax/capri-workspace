The latest approval selects the current source-matched panels and the original first Handoff comic as the layout foundation. This supersedes older V8 directions calling for one active character stage, Barlow Condensed italic display type, minimal abstract stand-ins or a stacked collision composition.

## Preserve the identity

- Approved hero/banner: byte-identical. Existing outlined banner lettering remains part of that approved asset.
- New presentation type: **Manrope 500** headings, **Inter 400/500** explanatory copy. Martian Mono 400 belongs to the original ASCII black-hole renderer. The font binaries and OFL notices are supplied.
- Ink #100a19, paper #f4f1ee, magenta #ff1265, green #28fb86; muted text must remain legible. Preserve the references' visual contrast and detail.
- No decorative eyebrow labels, footer slogans, italic condensed headings, giant rounded text boxes, unsolicited AI badges, fake telemetry or random dashboards.
- Capri is a full transparent character, with all hair tips, hands and feet retained. Several distinct models/poses should inhabit the explanations and art panels.
- Keep the first comic's staggered three scenes and concise meaningful end: findings return to the human, who decides. Captions and inspectable briefs carry the real explanation; the actor supplies gesture and personality.

## Responsive interpretation

At desktop GitHub widths, retain recognisable source composition and the first comic's three beats. At narrow widths, use properly composed portrait/static exports and clear native text rather than shrinking a wide wall of small lettering. Avoid clipping an actor to a decorative panel shape. A character may cross visual boundaries deliberately; its source rectangle must not become visible.

Preserve detail in the actual exported medium. Do not overcompress the ASCII geometry into generic speckles or make the hands/cat unreadable in order to meet an arbitrary file-size number.
